NACHO privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

NACHO is a desktop app for Windows and Linux. It runs on your own computer. It has no server and no account of its own, and its maker receives no data from it.

Pictures and files on your computer

NACHO reads the files you give it and writes smaller copies to a folder on your computer (Pictures\Docs-Ready unless you choose another). Nothing is uploaded, except as described under Google Docs below.

Google Docs

NACHO uses your Google account only when you choose "Shrink a Google Doc" and sign in. You sign in on Google's own page in your browser; NACHO never sees your password.

NACHO asks Google for one permission: "See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files that you use with this app" (drive.file). That covers:

  • the Google Doc you choose in Google's file picker, which NACHO reads;
  • a copy of that Doc, which Google makes in your Drive (same folder) when you choose "Into a new copy", the default. NACHO replaces each picture in the copy with a smaller one, keeping its place and size, and does not change the original. If you choose "Into this Doc" instead, NACHO replaces the pictures in the Doc itself; and
  • temporary picture files NACHO creates in your Drive. Each is shared as "anyone with the link" for a few seconds, so Google Docs can copy it into your Doc, and then deleted.

NACHO cannot see any other file in your Drive.

How the data is handled

  • The pictures of the Doc you choose are downloaded to your computer, made smaller there, and put into the copy (or, if you choose, back into the same Doc). NACHO never deletes a Doc.
  • If you choose (it is on by default), copies of the original pictures are kept on your computer, in Docs-Ready\Google Docs originals. You can delete them at any time.
  • Your sign-in (a refresh token) is stored only on your computer: encrypted for your Windows user, or on Linux in a file only your user can read. Signing out in NACHO deletes it and asks Google to revoke it.
  • No data from your Google account is sent anywhere else, sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train any AI or machine-learning model. NACHO keeps no other copy of your Docs.
  • NACHO's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can remove NACHO's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Update check

Once a day (you can turn it off in More options) NACHO asks GitHub's public API whether a newer release exists. The request carries only "NACHO/<version>" as its user agent.
